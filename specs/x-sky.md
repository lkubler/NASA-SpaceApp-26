# Day sky in the walk
The visitor should understand: I am standing under the real Martian sky, not in a grey box.
Rests on: `specs/4-walk.md`, the team's notes (the sky could be much better; no night sky, a good day sky instead).

Sky => a dome with a soft gradient in the butterscotch tones of Curiosity's daytime sky photos: dusty and brighter at the horizon, slightly darker overhead, brighter around the sun
Sun => a small disc with a soft glow, at a fixed place that fits the shadows in the ground photos; it does not move
Haze => the far ground and the skyline fade into the horizon colour of the sky, no visible seam between ground, skyline and sky
Other lines in the scene => the steep border, the radio line, the route and the beacon fade into the same haze colour
Reference => real Curiosity sky photos (day sky at Gale crater); the builder puts screenshots in four directions next to them in `reviews/`
Not allowed => Earth-like blue sky, a large sun, lens flares, clouds, stars
Label => "Sky: model" in the legend; the reference photos are listed on the Sources page
If it does not look better than the sky today => it is not merged; the sky stays as it is

Not in this spec: night, sunset, a moving sun, weather, dust storms
