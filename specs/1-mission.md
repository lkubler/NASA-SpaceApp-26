# 1 Mission
The visitor should understand: Earth asks for one sample, the answer takes minutes to arrive, so the crew plans this excursion alone.
Rests on: `research/research-requests.md`, `research/walk-targets.md`, `docs/mockups/01-choose-mission.png`

Opening => two sentences before anything else: a crew on Mars, 4 to 22 minutes of signal delay each way; one question: can I still get home?
View => Gale crater in 3D from real elevation data (MOLA or HRSC), filling the screen; Mount Sharp labelled; label: real
No crater-wide elevation data obtainable => the 4 km terrain around Murray Buttes in 3D instead, labelled as such
Headline => "Choose your mission." at the bottom left, one line of text below it
Sites => markers in the crater: Murray Buttes selectable; Marias Pass and Pahrump Hills shown with a lock, "not in this demo"
Selecting Murray Buttes => the requests from Earth appear as cards
Requests => two: the cross-beds in the sandstone of the buttes; the radiation next to a butte wall
Card => who asks, what sample or measurement, why it matters in one sentence, a real Curiosity photo
Wording => names the rock, not a point: "a sample from the sandstone at the foot of a butte"
One task => each request asks for exactly one sample or measurement
Signal delay => shown with the time the request was sent
Choosing => one request; the main button leads to Plan

Not in this spec: launch, landing, a base, rover drive, playable other sites, devices to place, the request about the mudstone contact (it cannot be placed in the terrain).
