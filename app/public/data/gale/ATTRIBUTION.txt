NASA MOLA altimetry, heights converted to metres (scripts/preprocess/build_gale_hrsc.mjs).

gale_color.rgb.bin: colours sampled from the NASA/JPL-Caltech "Planet Mars, 3D Model" texture.
Drive line and sites in gale_hrsc.json: NASA/JPL-Caltech, MSL_waypoints.json.
