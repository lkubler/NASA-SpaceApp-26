sev.glb
Source: NASA Science 3D Resources, "Space Exploration Vehicle", credit NASA/LaRC/Advanced Concepts Lab (Mar 20, 2025),
https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/space-exploration-vehicle/
File: glTF download Space Exploration Vehicle.glb (22.43 MB),
https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/cds/3d/resources/model/space-exploration-vehicle/Space%20Exploration%20Vehicle.glb
Renamed to sev.glb and compressed (textures 1024 px, unused texture copies removed, mesh simplified from 413,198 to
85,062 triangles, meshopt; commands in scripts/preprocess/README.md); not otherwise edited. At load time the app
scales it to 4.5 m length and replaces the material with a plain one using the same colour texture
(src/steps/4-walk/scene3d.js). The page gives no licence text of its own, only a link to the NASA brand and usage
guidelines. No NASA logo found in the textures.
