## Priority actions (read first)
1. **SEXTANT's 2009 precursor already computes "the most efficient path back home from any point along the path"** (Johnson et al., ICES 2009-01-2562, abstract V). So "return path" alone is not novel. Our differentiator must be wording like: return *reserve* (energy budget plus turn-back point) combined with comms shadow, load-scaled, in the browser on real Mars terrain. Do not claim "nobody has a way home function".
2. **Radiation number:** the Gale surface dose equivalent is 0.67 mSv/day (Hassler, SwRI release V), not 0.64. Absorbed dose 0.210 +/- 0.040 mGy/day (S). Fix any 0.64 anywhere.
3. **pextant repo not findable:** github.com/pextant and /pextant/pextant both return 404. A Python SEXTANT port exists (Norheim MIT thesis 2018, V abstract) but no license/repo confirmed. Do not promise reuse; plan to write our own (small).
4. **Honest framing:** no published suited, Mars-gravity, graded-slope metabolic regression exists (sources-validity.md). Say "model with uncertainty band; SEXTANT's own field test found it under-predicts time and energy".
