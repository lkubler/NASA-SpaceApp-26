## P1 (breaks or damages the demo)

**P1-1 "Move relay to high ground" freezes the page for about 4.4 s with no feedback, and gives no cue that the user should press it.** Measured: the click handler blocks the main thread 4.4 s (swiftshader CPU; a real laptop is maybe 1-3 s, still visible). Button does not change, no spinner, cursor does not change, and the screenshot taken 0.4 s later already shows the result only because the browser could not paint earlier. In a 30 s demo this is the money moment (comms shadow 115 min to 0 min) and a judge will think it hung and click again (a second click re-runs the whole 4 s). Also nothing in the default verdict ("Whole loop within budget ... 22 %") tells the user that the comms shadow of 115 min is the problem or that the button is the cure: the red "115 min longest" tile and the button sit apart. Shots: `03b-map-1280-settled.png`, `06-relay-done.png`. Fix: on click set the button to "Moving relay..." and disabled, yield one frame (`requestAnimationFrame` + `setTimeout 0`) before the compute, then pulse the shadow hatch fading out; add one sentence under the verdict when comms shadow > 0, e.g. "Longest time out of contact 115 min. The relay rover can't see you there. Try: Move relay to high ground." Owner: builder (busy state, yield), designer (copy, fade).

**P1-2 The map first shows a black canvas for the first seconds, and at the wrong size after a viewport change.** (a) Arriving from the briefing "Plan the EVA", the map area was still empty (only the dark brown background, the legend and the panel) 3 s after the route existed (`03-map.png`); the image tiles came later (`05-relay-400ms.png`, `03b`). On a slow judge laptop this reads as "broken, nothing loads" right after the briefing button. (b) After resizing the window from 1280x720 to 1920x1080 on the map page, the canvas stayed black / offset (`14-map-1080.png`: terrain squeezed at the left edge, 1500 px black). A fresh load at 1920x1080 is fine (`14b-map-1080-fresh.png`). Judges who drag a window to a projector or press F11 will get the black map. Fix: show a "Loading terrain imagery..." placeholder over the canvas until the ortho image is drawn; add a `resize` (ResizeObserver) handler that resizes the canvas and redraws, and also re-fit on first show. Owner: builder.

Owner: builder.
