Choose personas that fit the target users in `DECISIONS.md` (e.g. EVA planner, astronaut on the walk, a judge seeing it for 30 seconds). Screenshots go to `research/ux/`, findings to `research/ux-<time>.md`. Serious issues become tasks in `tasks/`. Visual or aesthetic issues go to `designer` and are checked against `docs/design.md`. In FREEZE, focus on the demo path. Commit your findings.
